About LOVE Rajkot Cab

LOVE Rajkot Cab is a family-run taxi service based in Rajkot. It started with a single sedan and a phone number passed between neighbours, and it still works the same way: one number, answered by the people who own the cars. Most of our work comes from repeat customers and their referrals.

We drive Rajkot city daily and the Saurashtra circuit constantly: Jamnagar, Junagadh, Gir, Porbandar, Dwarka, Somnath, Diu and Bhuj. Beyond Gujarat we run regularly to Mumbai, Udaipur and Mount Abu. Airport and railway transfers are a large part of what we do, at every hour.

Our cars are owned rather than sublet, so they are serviced on a schedule and cleaned between trips. Drivers are permanent, licensed, and know the Saurashtra highways well enough to plan around traffic, road work and darshan timings at the temple towns.

Two promises hold this business together. The car arrives at the time agreed, and the fare quoted before departure is the fare you pay. Everything else, from luggage to a change of route, we work out with you on the call.
